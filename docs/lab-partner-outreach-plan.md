# Private Lab Test Lab Partner Outreach Plan

## Goal

Build broad, low-cost lab access without pretending Private Lab Test already has direct national contracts. The first production-ready network should combine:

- One API-network partner for ordering, requisitions, result delivery, and state coverage.
- One mobile phlebotomy path for older adults and people who cannot easily travel.
- A regional independent lab pipeline that collects real cash-pay prices by state.
- A later national-lab track using real order volume and completion data as proof.

## 30-Day Operating Plan

### Days 1-3: API Coverage

Targets: Junction, LabTestingAPI, ORDRS, Health Gorilla.

Ask for:

- Sandbox or technical documentation.
- State coverage and restricted-state rules.
- Common wellness test menu and cash pricing.
- Physician authorization terms.
- Requisition generation and result delivery examples.
- Critical-value escalation workflow.

Success condition: one technical discovery call booked and one partner scored as a likely MVP adapter replacement.

### Days 4-10: Mobile Draw Access

Targets: Getlabs plus one local mobile phlebotomy provider per launch state.

Ask for:

- ZIP-level service coverage.
- Collection fees and cancellation fees.
- Specimen handling and lab drop-off rules.
- Scheduling API, portal, or concierge workflow.
- Recollection policy.

Success condition: at least one mobile collection workflow is documented well enough to quote pricing and turnaround honestly.

### Days 11-20: Regional Cash Menus

Targets: 25 CLIA-certified labs per launch state.

Launch state order:

1. Colorado
2. Texas
3. Florida
4. California
5. Georgia
6. North Carolina

Ask each lab for:

- Written cash-pay menu.
- Patient service center addresses and appointment rules.
- Whether they accept clinician-authorized third-party requisitions.
- Result delivery method.
- Critical-value escalation policy.
- BAA and security requirements.

Success condition: two written cash menus and one regional workflow call booked.

### Days 21-30: Enterprise Proof Packet

Targets: Quest Health, Labcorp, Sonic Healthcare USA divisions, BioReference.

Use proof points:

- Orders attempted by ZIP and state.
- Tests requested most often.
- Abandoned checkouts caused by no draw access.
- Support requests from older adults or family caregivers.
- Completion rate from existing partners.
- Willingness to pay for mobile draw.

Success condition: one national or regional enterprise conversation opened with real demand data.

## Qualification Checklist

Do not activate a partner until these are documented:

- CLIA certificate and state availability.
- Cash-pay menu with lab fees and draw fees separated.
- Ordering model that supports clinician authorization and no insurance billing.
- Requisition workflow that a patient and concierge can follow.
- Result delivery format: API, SFTP, portal export, or secure PDF.
- Critical values route to the ordering clinician before release to the customer.
- Patient service center or mobile draw coverage is mapped by ZIP.
- BAA, privacy, security, and audit-log expectations are understood.

Track every Friday:

- 50 new lab leads researched.
- 25 first-touch emails sent.
- 10 phone calls or form submissions completed.
- 5 partner packets sent.
- 2 pricing menus received.
- 1 technical or contracting call booked.

# Telehealth Lab Membership Roadmap

Date: May 8, 2026

## Product Thesis

Private Lab Test should not become only a cheaper catalog of tests. The long-term product should become a private lab-first telehealth membership:

1. Order affordable self-pay labs.
2. Understand results in a calm dashboard.
3. Get clinician interpretation when needed.
4. Retest on a useful cadence.
5. Move into optional programs or treatment partners only where licensed clinical operations support it.

That is the shape that can make the company feel like a lab-testing version of MEDVi, Hims, Function, and Superpower without rushing into unsafe medical claims.

## The Advice Boundary

The platform should use three separate layers:

### 1. Educational Insights

Can launch before a clinician network is fully live.

- Explain what each marker generally means.
- Show high, low, normal, and trend direction.
- Suggest questions to ask a clinician.
- Recommend common retest intervals when appropriate.

Must not:

- Diagnose.
- Tell someone to start, stop, or change medication.
- Replace urgent care or a licensed clinician.

### 2. Licensed Clinician Review

Requires a contracted clinician network, state matching, documentation, protocols, and privacy/compliance review.

- Clinician reviews abnormal or confusing results.
- Clinician answers lab-result questions.
- Clinician recommends follow-up labs or primary-care follow-up.
- Critical values are handled by the ordering provider or clinical partner workflow.

### 3. Future Treatment Pathways

Only after the clinical layer is real.

- Metabolic and weight-management pathways.
- Hormone and thyroid pathways.
- Cardiology, fertility, menopause, or primary-care referrals.
- Medication-safety lab monitoring when a licensed provider orders it.

The product should never auto-prescribe from labs or imply treatment without a provider relationship.

## Membership Tiers To Build Toward

### Lab Pass Basic

The simple annual private-health check.

- One broad panel per year.
- Result dashboard and educational insights.
- Retest reminders.
- Support for prep, locations, and result timing.
- Clear renewal notice and easy cancellation.

### Lab Pass Plus

Labs plus interpretation.

- Everything in Basic.
- One clinician result-review credit.
- Priority support.
- Follow-up lab suggestions from a licensed clinician where available.

### Metabolic Tracking

The MEDVi/Hims-style path, but labs-first.

- Baseline panel.
- 12-week retest.
- 6-month monitoring.
- Optional clinician consult.
- No prescription workflow until a partner clinician owns the medical decision.

### Family Care

Future permissioned support.

- Adult caregiver access.
- Shared reminders.
- Visit prep and support escalation.
- Audit logs and revocable consent.

## Retention Loops

1. Result-release loop
   - Trigger: results are ready.
   - Value: plain-language summary, trend snapshot, and next-step options.

2. Out-of-range follow-up loop
   - Trigger: abnormal marker.
   - Value: calm explanation plus clinician-review option.

3. Retest cadence loop
   - Trigger: 30, 90, 180, and 365 day intervals depending on panel.
   - Value: one-click reorder and trend comparison.

4. Program progress loop
   - Trigger: user joins a metabolic, hormone, heart, or baseline track.
   - Value: timeline, milestones, and a reason to come back.

5. Member savings loop
   - Trigger: repeat purchase intent or renewal window.
   - Value: transparent member pricing and priority support.

## What Changes In The Current Plan

- Ulta and Quest are now the near-term real-ordering path if approved.
- Junction remains the API sandbox/production-integration path.
- ORDRS is paused as a later backup because onboarding is currently too circular.
- A clinician infrastructure partner becomes a core requirement before any medical-advice or treatment language goes live.
- Subscription design starts now, but live subscription pricing waits until real provider costs, fulfillment cost, support load, and partner economics are known.
